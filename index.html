⏰ Simple Past & Past Continuous Tense

The Simple Past Tense is used to talk about actions completed at a specific time in the past. The Past Continuous Tense describes actions that were ongoing at a specific moment in the past (Murphy, 2019; Swan, 2016).

🟢 Regular Verbs (-ed)

Regular past tense verbs are formed by adding -ed or -d to the base form (Swan, 2016).

  • Most verbs: add -ed (walk → walked)
  • Verbs ending in -e: add -d (like → liked)
  • Consonant + y: change y to -ied (study → studied)
  • One short vowel + one consonant: double the consonant (stop → stopped)

🌊 Past Continuous: was / were + verb-ing

Use the past continuous for an action that was in progress at a certain time in the past, or while something else happened (Murphy, 2019).

  • was with I, he, she, it
  • were with you, we, they and plural nouns (the children were playing)
  • -ing spelling: walk → walking, write → writing (drop the e), run → running (double the consonant)
✅ Positive She was walking to school.
🚫 Negative They weren't playing football.
❓ Question Were you studying at 8 p.m.?

📞 Interrupted action: past continuous + simple past

The long action uses the past continuous. The short action that interrupts it uses the simple past.

    📘 User Manual & Courseware Guide

    This user manual explains how children, teachers and parents can use the courseware.

    1. System Requirements & Setup

    • Runs in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, Firefox).

    • Turn on the device's sound. Press Start on the first screen to begin, with or without the background music.

    • An internet connection is needed for the videos in the Fun Zone.

    2. Navigation Controls

    • Use the coloured buttons at the top to switch between Lessons, Fun Zone, Games, Quiz Time, User Manual and References. With a keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between them.

    • Press 🔊 Listen to hear a word or sentence read slowly. Tap Tico the turtle to hear him say hello.

    3. Sound Settings (🎛️ Sound button)

    • Music: quiet, calm background sounds (soft chords, little bells, a gentle breeze and birds). You can turn it on or off and change the volume. The music gets quieter by itself while a word is being read.

    • Sound effects: the little dings and pops. Turn them off if you want it quieter.

    • Reading voice: spoken words come from the text-to-speech voices built into your browser (Liu, 2026). The courseware picks the most natural-sounding voice on your device and reads slowly. You can choose a different voice from the list and press “Try this voice”. The voices available depend on the device and browser, so they can sound different on each one.

    4. Learning Activities

    • Study the Lessons, then watch the video lessons in the Fun Zone. Press Watch here to play a video on the page, or Open on YouTube to watch it in a new tab. Some places do not allow videos inside the page, so the YouTube button always works.

    • Unjumble the Sentence (Games): drag the words into the boxes to build a sentence, or tap a word to send it to the next empty box. With a keyboard, press Enter on a word to place it, and use the left and right arrow keys to move a placed word. Press Check when every box is full. You earn a star when a sentence is right on the first check.

    • Word Sorter (Games): drag each card into the Normal words or the Past tense words basket, or tap a card and then tap a basket. Wrong cards wiggle and stay for another try. The score counts the cards you sorted right on the first try.

    • Answer all 6 questions in Quiz Time. After each answer you see the correct answer and a short explanation.

    • When a quiz or game is finished, your score is shown. If you get about two thirds or more right, a celebration animation and sound play. Press Try again or Play again to restart with the words shuffled.

    5. Accessibility

    • The main text and button colours meet the WCAG 2.1 level AA contrast minimum of 4.5:1 (Kirkpatrick et al., 2025).

    • Buttons, quiz answers and both games can be used with a keyboard. Right and wrong answers are shown with words and symbols as well as colour. Animations and confetti are switched off when the device is set to reduce motion.
